Announcer Audio

Speaks each line aloud as it streams in, so you can race without reading the overlay. Uses your Mac's built-in voices — no extra API cost and no network round trip. The volume_up button in the main window mutes it quickly.

Tip: Apple's free Enhanced/Premium voices sound noticeably better at the same speed — System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Manage Voices.

AI Provider

Commentary is generated by a model you supply a key for. Usage is billed to your account with that provider.

Model IDs are free text because they change faster than this mod ships — paste whatever your service currently offers. The cost fields are optional and only drive the spend readout; leave them blank and cost shows as untracked.

Signing in with Discord gives you a bigger monthly allowance and a key that survives a reinstall — sign in again anywhere and you get the same one back. Connecting anonymously works without an account, but the allowance is smaller and clearing your settings loses it.

The free tier provides the announcer voices above — your own prompts from the Prompts tab are not used. No API key is needed and nothing is billed to you. Rider data, including the names and power of riders around you, is sent to the service.

Getting Started

Pick one of three ways to get commentary, on the AI Provider tab.

  1. Lunatic hosted — no API key. Paste the service URL, click Connect, and pick a model and a voice. A monthly allowance of free calls, nothing billed to you. Your own prompts from the Prompts tab are not used on this option.
  2. Anthropic. Sign up at console.anthropic.com, create a key, paste it, and choose a model. Usage is billed to your account.
  3. OpenAI-compatible. Works with OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq and a local Ollama. Choose the service, paste a key, and enter a model id. Model ids are free text because they change often — paste whatever your service currently offers.

Then click Test Connection (or Connect), choose a style on the Prompts tab, and ride. The commentary fires on what happens on the road, not on a timer.

Commentary Styles

  • Tour de France: A television race call. One punchy line as things happen on the road.
  • Tactical Coach: Direct advice on when to attack, who to watch, and how to respond.
  • Custom: Write your own system and user prompts. Not available on the hosted provider, which supplies its own voices.

On the hosted provider the voice is chosen on the AI Provider tab instead, and the selection here is ignored.

Cost Information

On the hosted provider you pay nothing — the readout shows free calls remaining instead of a price.

Anthropic pricing, per 1K tokens:

  • Haiku 4.5: ~$0.001 input, ~$0.005 output
  • Sonnet 5: ~$0.002 input, ~$0.010 output
  • Opus 5: ~$0.005 input, ~$0.025 output

A request sends about 1,000 tokens and gets back about 60. On Haiku that is roughly $0.0013 a call, or $0.10–0.39 per hour of racing depending on how eventful it is.

For an OpenAI-compatible service the mod has no price list, so the cost readout shows an asterisk until you enter the per-million rates on the AI Provider tab.

Tips

  • Use Haiku for best speed and lowest cost - it's perfect for live commentary.
  • Reduce max tokens for shorter, punchier commentary.
  • Select fewer data fields to focus commentary on what matters most to you.
  • The pause button in the main window stops auto-updates but keeps history.
  • Click copy to share particularly good commentary.
